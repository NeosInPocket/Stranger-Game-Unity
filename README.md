# Stranger

2D action RPG built in Unity by a team of three. The player explores a map and caves, completes quests and fights through to a final boss.

## Features

- Guns with attachments: pistol, shotgun, AK47, M1A1 and AWP; laser sight, gun stock and extended magazine.
- Inventory items: chests, potions, armour and ammo.
- Quests and NPC dialogues.
- Enemy AI with aggro distance and a multi-stage boss.

## My part

I made 27 of the project's 60 commits, mainly on:

- player controller, item pickup and interaction (`Character/Controller`);
- enemies and the boss fight (`Enemy`);
- the final scene and comet effects.

## Run it

Open `Project/New Unity Project` in Unity 2021.3.8f1 and start from the `Menu` scene.

## Stack

Unity, C#, Cinemachine
